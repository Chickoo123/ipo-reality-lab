J / BUILD & QUALITY

Evidence-gated development.

Beginner-friendly static-site stack. Real-data ingestion is manually reviewed and automated checks stop unsupported data from shipping.

Data workflow

  1. Download the dated official filing for internal review.
  2. Extract the document independently twice.
  3. Compare the extractions and resolve mismatches against the PDF.
  4. Validate allocation totals, cumulative use, revisions and evidence links.
  5. Publish JSON only after a human page-level review.

Quality gates

  • Every figure has a document ID and page.
  • RHP allocations remain immutable baselines.
  • Cumulative utilisation cannot fall without a recorded revision.
  • Utilisation cannot exceed allocation without supported revision.
  • No schedule disclosed = no K2 schedule status.
  • Unknown facts remain missing, not estimated.
