# Competitor and overlap audit

**Snapshot: 30 October 2026.** Public website review only; not exhaustive. Features, availability and terms can change. Re-check before external differentiation claims.

| Product / source | Confirmed public overlap | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| SEBI / NSE / BSE | Offer documents, public issue information, exchange disclosures, regulatory and investor-education material | Treat as primary-source destinations and official infrastructure. |
| Trendlyne | Upcoming/open/recent IPO dashboard, DRHP/RHP links, category subscription and gain/loss analysis | Basic A-style tracking is saturated. |
| BeaconCaps | IPO Insights, forensic prospectus reads, use-of-proceeds coverage and a post-listing accountability tracker | K1/K2 are not unique. Compete on standardised cross-IPO object/quarter data, preserved RHP baseline, page-level evidence and reproducible validation. |
| CredoNomics | Public quantitative IPO score methodology using growth, returns, cash conversion, leverage and peer valuation | Do not build a black-box score. Show raw inputs and formulas. |
| IPOWatch | IPO calendar, current/upcoming issues, GMP, subscription and allotment tracking | Do not create another live-GMP clone. |
| GMP Today | Multi-source unofficial GMP aggregation, timestamped observations and historical accuracy material | Optional only after licence, attribution, method and SEBI review; not official exchange data. |
| SEBI Investor Education | Investor education and offer-document guidance | Academy must add applied source-linked lessons using IPO Reality Lab's research model, not copy generic explainers. |

## Strategic position

The market is not empty. The strongest credible proposition is a repeatable evidence workflow connecting:
- exact RHP object baseline,
- subsequent quarterly utilisation by object,
- disclosed deployment schedule,
- approved revisions and filed comments,
- document-version changes,
- seller-level OFS detail,
- automated validation and human page-level review.

This is more defensible than calling K1–K4 exclusive, but is still a hypothesis to validate against competitor products and actual coverage quality.
