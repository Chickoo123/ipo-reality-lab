## Issue structure (prospectus printed page 1)

| Item | Source value | Converted value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total issue | ₹4,928.80 million | ₹492.88 crore |
| Fresh issue | ₹4,000.00 million | ₹400.00 crore |
| OFS | ₹928.80 million | ₹92.88 crore |
| Offer price | ₹172 per share | ₹172 per share |

The issue split reconciles: ₹400.00 crore + ₹92.88 crore = ₹492.88 crore.

The promoter selling shareholder shown in the prospectus is Rajendra Sethia, offering 5,400,000 shares. The prospectus shows WACA of ₹5.00 per share, certified by Abhijit Dutt & Associates. The simple issue-price / WACA calculation is 34.4x; this is a derived ratio and must not be confused with a valuation multiple or an investment conclusion.

## Net proceeds and objects (prospectus printed page 100)

The prospectus states fresh-issue gross proceeds of ₹4,000.00 million, offer-related expenses of ₹370.62 million and net proceeds of ₹3,629.38 million.

| Object | Allocation (₹ million) | Allocation (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Prepayment / scheduled repayment of borrowings | 1,635.00 | 163.500 |
| Capital expenditure (vehicles, containers, reach stackers) | 1,517.10 | 151.710 |
| General corporate purposes | 477.28 | 47.728 |
| **Total net proceeds** | **3,629.38** | **362.938** |

The object allocations reconcile exactly to the disclosed net proceeds. The prospectus shows an annual deployment schedule across FY2025, FY2026 and FY2027 for the capex object.

## Interpretation limits

- The offer-document schedule is annual, while the cited monitoring report is an interim snapshot at 30 September 2024. We do **not** label an object ahead, on schedule or behind based on this comparison alone.
- “No comments” is not proof that a project was completed or that its outcome was successful.
- CRISIL states in its printed page 10 disclaimer that it relies on issuer-management and third-party information and does not perform an audit or independent verification. That caveat belongs alongside the data.
- The allocation baseline currently cites the final prospectus pages 98–100. The original RHP link is recorded, but the exact RHP page cross-check remains pending.
- Values are converted from ₹ million to ₹ crore by dividing by 10. The source values and source units are preserved in the research notes / official PDF.
